# Support replies

How to use this file:

- Reply from support@rollcal.app, to the address the person wrote from.
- Keep the reference (for example #C7EA6E60) in the subject as "Re: [#REF] ...".
- Replace every [bracket] before sending, and delete lines that don't apply.
- Aim to reply within 5-7 business days. Abuse or child-safety reports: act within 24 hours.
- Topics in the app: Account change, Problem with the app, RollCal Pro, Something else.

## 6. Forgot password / change password

Use when: someone asks how to reset or change their password.

Subject: Re: [#REF] Your password

Hi [First name],

Here's how to get a new password.

If you're signed in: open Edit profile, then Password, and choose a new one.

If you're signed out: on the sign-in screen tap "Forgot password?". We'll email a 6-digit code from confirm@rollcal.app. Enter it in the app and choose a new password. Please check spam and use the newest code.

If the code doesn't arrive, reply and we'll help.

## 15. Bill or payment dispute between users

Use when: two people disagree about who owes what.

Subject: Re: [#REF] Your bill split

Hi [First name],

Thanks for explaining. RollCal only records who paid and who owes whom. No money moves through the app, so we can't take payments, refund anyone or settle a disagreement between people.

What you can do in the app:

- Open the event's bill and edit the amount or who it's split between if something is wrong.
- Mark a payment as paid once it has been settled outside the app, or change it back if it was marked by mistake.

For the money itself, it's best to sort it out directly with the other person.

RollCal Support
