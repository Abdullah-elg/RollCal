Delete your account

In the app: open Settings, then tap Delete account at the bottom of the Account section. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app: email support@rollcal.app from the address you signed up with, and we’ll delete it within 30 days.

What’s deleted

Your profile, your messages, photos and videos you uploaded, events you created and everything in them, your friends list, bills and payment details. Copies other people already saved to their own phones stay with them.

See the Privacy Policy for more.
