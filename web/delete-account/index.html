Delete your account

Last updated November 27, 2026

You can delete your RollCal account and everything linked to it at any time.

In the app

Open Settings, then tap Delete account at the bottom of the Account section. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email support@rollcal.app from the address you signed up with, and we’ll delete it within 30 days.

If you pay for a plan

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a RollCal Plus or Pro subscription. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Event Passes end with the events they were bought for, and those are deleted with your account.

What’s deleted

Your profile, your messages, photos and videos you uploaded and your reactions, events you created and everything in them (including their video recaps and any Event Passes), your friends list, bills and payment details, support requests and notification settings. A video recap of someone else’s event that includes your photos is deleted too.

What’s kept

Reports other people made about you are kept so we can keep RollCal safe, but they’re no longer linked to your account. Emails you sent us stay in our support inbox. Copies in our backups are overwritten within 30 days. Photos and videos other people already saved to their own phones stay with them.

Delete some of your data and keep your account

You can delete these yourself in the app at any time:

  • Photos and videos you added to an event roll: open the photo, tap ⋯, then Delete photo.
  • Reactions: open the photo and tap your reaction again to remove it.
  • Bills you created: open the bill, then Delete bill.
  • Events you host: open the event, then Cancel event. This deletes everything in it.
  • Events you joined: leave the event.
  • Receiving methods (Interac, PayPal and so on): Settings, Receiving methods, then Remove.
  • Friends: remove them from your friends list.
  • Profile photo: Edit profile, then Remove photo.

For anything else, such as chat messages, email support@rollcal.app from the address you signed up with and say what you’d like deleted. We’ll do it within 30 days.
