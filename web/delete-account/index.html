RollCal

Delete your account

In the app: open Settings, then tap Delete account at the bottom of the Account section. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app: email support@rollcal.app from the address you signed up with, and we’ll delete it within 30 days.

What’s deleted

Your profile, your messages, photos and videos you uploaded, events you created and everything in them, your friends list, bills and payment details, support requests and notification settings.

What’s kept

Reports other people made about you are kept so we can keep RollCal safe, but they’re no longer linked to your account. Emails you sent us stay in our support inbox. Copies in our backups are overwritten within 30 days. Photos other people already saved to their own phones stay with them.

Delete some of your data and keep your account

You can delete these yourself in the app at any time:

  • Photos and videos you added to an event roll: open the photo, then Delete photo.
  • Bills you created: open the bill, then Delete bill.
  • Events you host: open the event, then Cancel event. This deletes everything in it.
  • Events you joined: leave the event.
  • Receiving methods (Interac, PayPal and so on): Settings, Receiving methods, then Remove.
  • Friends: remove them from your friends list.
  • Profile photo: Edit profile, then Remove photo.

For anything else, such as chat messages, email support@rollcal.app from the address you signed up with and say what you’d like deleted. We’ll do it within 30 days.
