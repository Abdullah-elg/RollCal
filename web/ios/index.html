Get RollCal on your iPhone

RollCal is in beta, so it comes through TestFlight, Apple’s free app for trying new apps before they’re in the App Store.

  • Do this on your iPhone
  • About 2 minutes
  • Free

On a computer? Open rollcal.app/ios on your iPhone instead. The buttons below only work there.

  1. Install TestFlight

    Tap the button and then Get in the App Store. If TestFlight is already on your phone, go to step 2.

    Get TestFlight

  2. Accept the invite

    Come back to this page and tap the button. TestFlight opens on the RollCal page. Tap Accept, then Install.

    Join the RollCal beta

    If the button opens a web page instead, tap View in TestFlight on it.

  3. Open RollCal

    RollCal is on your Home Screen now, with a small orange dot by its name while it’s a beta. Open it, then sign in or make an account.

Having trouble?

“This beta is full” or “isn’t accepting testers”

We’ve hit the tester limit for now. Email support@rollcal.app and we’ll add you when a spot opens.

The button doesn’t open TestFlight

Make sure TestFlight is installed (step 1), then open this page in Safari and tap the button again.

How do I get updates?

New versions show up in TestFlight. Open TestFlight, tap RollCal and turn on Automatic Updates to get them straight away.

How do I send feedback?

Take a screenshot while using RollCal and tap Share Beta Feedback, or email support@rollcal.app.
