RollCal beta for iPhone

RollCal is in beta. On iPhone you get it through TestFlight, Apple’s app for testing new apps. It takes about two minutes. Do these steps on your iPhone.

  1. Get TestFlight

    Install Apple’s free TestFlight app from the App Store.

    TestFlight on the App Store

  2. Join the RollCal beta

    Tap the button below. It opens TestFlight. Tap Accept, then Install.

    Join the beta

  3. Open RollCal

    RollCal is now on your Home Screen. Sign in or make an account and you’re set.

Good to know

New versions show up in TestFlight. Turn on automatic updates there to get them straight away.

Found a bug or have an idea? Take a screenshot in RollCal and TestFlight lets you send it to us with a note, or email support@rollcal.app.
