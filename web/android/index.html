RollCal beta for Android

RollCal is in beta, so Google Play only shows it to testers. Use the same Google account that’s on your phone for every step.

  1. Join the testers group

    Open the group and tap Join group. This is what lets Google Play show you the app.

    Join the testers group

  2. Become a tester

    Open this page and tap Become a tester.

    Become a tester

  3. Download RollCal

    Install it from Google Play like any other app. If it says the app isn’t available, wait a few minutes after step 2 and try again.

    Get it on Google Play

Good to know

Updates come through Google Play like any other app.

Please keep RollCal installed while you’re testing. Found a bug or have an idea? Email support@rollcal.app, or leave private feedback on the RollCal page in Google Play.

On iPhone? Get the iPhone beta.
