Get RollCal on your Android phone

RollCal is in beta, so Google Play only shows it to testers. These steps add you as a tester, then you install it like any other app.

  • Do this on your phone
  • About 3 minutes
  • Free

On a computer? Open rollcal.app/android on your phone instead, so every step uses the Google account on it.

  1. Join the testers group

    Tap the button, then Join group. This is what lets Google Play show you RollCal.

    Join the testers group

    Use the same Google account that’s signed in to Google Play on your phone. If it asks you to sign in, check it’s that one.

  2. Become a tester

    Come back to this page, tap the button, then tap Become a tester.

    Become a tester

  3. Install RollCal

    Tap the button and then Install in Google Play. Open RollCal, then sign in or make an account.

    Open in Google Play

Having trouble?

Google Play says the app isn’t available

It can take a few minutes after step 2. Wait a little, close Google Play completely, then try step 3 again.

I have more than one Google account

Every step has to use the same account. In Google Play, tap your profile picture at the top right and switch to the account you joined the group with, then try step 3 again.

“Become a tester” page says something went wrong

Make sure you joined the group in step 1 with the same account, then try again. Still stuck? Email support@rollcal.app.

How do I get updates and send feedback?

Updates come through Google Play like any other app. Please keep RollCal installed while you’re testing. For feedback, email support@rollcal.app or leave private feedback on the RollCal page in Google Play.
