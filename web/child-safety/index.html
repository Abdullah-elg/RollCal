Child Safety Standards

Last updated November 27, 2026

RollCal has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to everyone who uses RollCal and to everything shared in it: profiles, photos, videos, video recaps and their captions, event details and messages.

What is not allowed

You may not use RollCal to:

  • share, request or link to sexual content involving anyone under 18, including drawn, edited or AI-generated images
  • groom, sexualise, sexually exploit or sextort a minor, or try to arrange contact with a minor for sexual purposes
  • promote, praise or normalise the sexual abuse of children
  • traffic or advertise children for any purpose

RollCal is for people aged 13 and over. We ask for your birth month and year when you sign up, and delete accounts we learn belong to anyone under 13.

How to report

In the app, you can report any person, message, photo, video or event. Tap the person, open the photo or video and tap ⋯, use the event’s ⋯ menu, or long-press a message, then choose Report. For a child safety report, pick Nudity or sexual content and say in the details that a child is involved. You can also block anyone, which stops them from contacting you.

You can also email support@rollcal.app. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police first.

What we do

  • Every report is sent to our team and reviewed within 24 hours. Reports involving children are handled first.
  • We remove CSAE content as soon as we find it and permanently close the accounts involved.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant authorities, as the law requires, and keep the information they need for their investigation.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement investigating child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Following the law

RollCal follows the child safety laws that apply to it, including the duty to report CSAM to the authorities.

Contact

Our contact for child safety and CSAM questions, from users, law enforcement or app stores, is support@rollcal.app. Put “Child safety” in the subject line so it is seen first.
